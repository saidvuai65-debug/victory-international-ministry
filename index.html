01 | ABOUT THE MINISTRY

A Ministry Rooted in Faith, Fellowship & Service

Victory International Ministry is a Christian religious ministry based in Zanzibar. The ministry is involved in church and faith-related activities, with a focus on worship, prayer, evangelism, Bible teaching, Christian gatherings and community service.

The ministry provides opportunities for people to come together in Christian fellowship, worship God, learn from the Bible and strengthen their faith. Through its activities, the ministry seeks to encourage spiritual growth and promote love, unity and care for others.

church

Our Purpose

The purpose of Victory International Ministry is to serve God and share the Christian faith. The ministry seeks to support believers in their spiritual journey and create opportunities for worship, prayer, learning and fellowship.

groups

Who We Serve

The ministry's activities are intended for people who wish to participate in Christian worship, prayer, Bible learning, evangelism and community-oriented service. Specific programmes and participation arrangements can be described further by the ministry.

menu_book

Our Identity

As a Christian ministry, Victory International Ministry places the teachings of Jesus Christ and the Word of God at the centre of its religious activities. It aims to carry out its work with faith, humility, compassion and respect.
